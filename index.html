Mofwah — AI-Powered Voice Agent for Call Center Automation

Backend Developer · 5-member team · King Saud University

A high-availability platform that automates 24/7 call center operations — inbound and outbound support, sales, and appointment scheduling — with AI voice agents that sync with external CRM systems.

  • Architected a distributed Spring Boot microservices backend with independent Agent, Workflow, Phone and Campaign services.
  • Decoupled telephony handshakes behind a Facade pattern plus REST/Webhook architecture for seamless local telecom integration.
  • Built a financial and subscription engine on Stripe with webhook-driven state management, removing manual billing state tracking.
  • Hardened production reliability with Spring Cloud Gateway routing, Resilience4j fault tolerance and secure NAT-gateway egress to ElevenLabs and Twilio.
  • Containerized with Docker Compose, backed by PostgreSQL and Redis; validated with Postman and JUnit.
